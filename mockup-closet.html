Interactive design examples · not the live app

A closet, not a floating window.

The character gets the stage. Your equipped items stay in a head-to-feet rail. Open a slot to swap it, or expand the whole closet—always docked, with the silver frames and inventory details of old-school MapleStory.

01 · CLASSIC INVENTORY Compact rail / dense grid

Closer to the game.

A narrow equip strip and small, beveled inventory cells. The dock opens beside the character; item names appear on hover or keyboard focus.
